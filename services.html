Our Services

Precision Field Delivery Across the Midwest

Backed by 10+ years of direct field experience, our crews operate Vermeer directional drilling rigs, vibratory plows, compact trenchers, and vacuum excavation units to install underground utilities on time and to exact municipal specifications.

Core Specialty • Midwest Wide

Telecommunications & Fiber Optics

We construct high-capacity fiber optic conduit corridors and telecommunications backbones for broadband providers, enterprise utility networks, and municipal connectivity. From long-haul conduit runs to dense urban right-of-ways, our teams ensure continuous, code-compliant pathway placement.

  • High-density polyethylene (HDPE) conduit & multi-duct bundles
  • Microduct and micro-trenching for urban broadband expansion
  • Handhole, manhole, pull box, and equipment vault installation
  • Cable pulling, rodder support, and pre-strung conduit placement
  • Intercity arterial fiber routes and rural broadband initiatives
Midwest Wide Infrastructure

Horizontal Directional Drilling (HDD) & Trenching

Our trenchless directional drilling capabilities enable conduit and utility placement beneath busy roadways, rail lines, waterways, parking lots, and sensitive lawns without disturbing the surface. Guided by precision transmitter depth locators, our drillers steer through complex soil conditions with millimeter accuracy.

  • Trenchless road, railway, highway, and waterway crossings
  • Precision electronic walk-over locating with continuous depth profiling
  • Steerable tooling for clay, silt, compacted gravel, and dense ground
  • Open trenching, rock sawing, potholing, and hydro excavation
  • Minimal-footprint bore pits with immediate backfill and compaction
Dedicated Drop Bury Crews

Fiber Drop Bury Services

We deploy specialized drop bury crews equipped with low-ground-pressure tracked vibratory plows and compact micro-trenchers to deliver clean, last-mile fiber drops to homes, multi-dwelling units (MDUs), and commercial buildings. We prioritize minimal turf disturbance, zero utility strikes, and clean lawn restoration.

  • Residential and commercial last-mile fiber drop placement
  • Tracked vibratory plowing that cuts a clean slit with virtually no surface disruption
  • Compact mini-trenching for tight fence gates, narrow side yards, and garden beds
  • Rigorous Illinois 811 locate adherence before any tool touches the ground
  • Same-day ground compaction, seam tamping, and clean turf restoration

Project Process

How We Work from Start to Finish

We handle every project with direct communication, experienced crews, and clean restoration from initial utility locating to final cleanup.

01

Pre Construction & Staging

Engineering print review, utility locate verification, potholing crossing points, and equipment staging.

02

Precision Trenchless Boring

Steering the drill head along the engineered pitch profile while continuously logging depth data.

03

Conduit, Pipe & Fiber Pullback

Back reaming to required borehole diameter, attaching swivel assemblies, and pulling pipe or conduit bundles into place.

04

Final Tie In & Restoration

Setting access vaults to final grade, proof testing conduits, backfilling pits with mechanical compaction, and returning the surface to original condition.
