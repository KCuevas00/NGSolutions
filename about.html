Safety & Prevention

Safety First: Always Calling 811

Working underground leaves zero room for error. Before any drill head enters the ground, our crews ensure 811 utility locates are fully cleared and physically verify line depths. We follow strict safety practices on every job to protect existing utilities, our crew, and the jobsite.

  • Safety-trained operators & daily jobsite hazard checks
  • Mandatory 811 locate clearance & visual depth verification
  • Heavy-duty certified HDPE conduit materials
  • Accurate bore tracking logs & depth inspection on every shot

Project Process

How We Work from Start to Finish

We handle every project with direct communication, experienced operators, and clean restoration from the first drill shot to final cleanup.

01

Plan & Locate

Thorough plan review, mandatory 811 utility mark verification, visual potholing to confirm existing lines, and precise bore path mapping.

02

Guided Boring (HDD)

Trenchless directional boring using steerable drill heads with walk-over locator tracking, navigating beneath obstacles and roadways with zero surface destruction.

03

Conduit & Vault Placement

Pullback of certified HDPE bundles, multi-duct placement, pull-tape installation ready for carrier lines, and grade-level polymer vault setting.

04

Compaction & Restoration

Thorough mechanical backfill compaction, clean asphalt/turf restoration, and complete site clean-up leaving jobsites spotless.
