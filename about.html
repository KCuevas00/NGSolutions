Quality & Safety Standards

100% 811 Compliance & Rigorous Safety Benchmarks

Operating beneath congested utility corridors demands zero tolerance for error. Our crews operate under strict OSHA 1910.268 (Telecommunications) and OSHA 1926 (Excavation) safety standards. Prior to any drill head penetrating the earth, we execute mandatory 811 utility markings and vacuum potholing to physically locate and protect all cross-utilities. Every installation conforms to ASTM compaction ratings and municipal engineering criteria.

  • OSHA 10/30 safety certified operators & daily hazard audits
  • Mandatory 811 locate clearance & vacuum potholing verification
  • Certified SDR-11 / Schedule 40/80 HDPE conduit materials
  • Digital bore tracking logs & depth inspection documentation

Operational Framework

How We Work: Turnkey 4-Stage Project Execution

We manage every phase of underground utility deployment with single-source accountability, ensuring rapid turnaround, transparent communication, and immaculate site restoration for civil engineers, municipalities, and telecom providers.

01

Plan & Locate

Thorough plan sheet analysis, 811 utility mark verification, vacuum potholing to visually verify existing lines, and precise bore profile mapping.

02

Guided Boring (HDD)

Trenchless directional boring using digitized steerable drill heads, navigating beneath obstacles and roadways with zero surface destruction.

03

Conduit & Vault Placement

Pullback of certified HDPE bundles, multi-duct placement, cable pulling/jetting, and placement of grade-level polymer concrete vaults.

04

Compaction & Restoration

Rigorous mechanical compaction testing, clean asphalt/concrete patching, and complete turf re-grading leaving jobsites spotless.
