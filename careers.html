Lead HDD Drill Rig Operator

Full Time

Operate Vermeer directional drills on planned fiber and utility corridors. Responsible for steering bore heads, reading pitch and roll, mixing bentonite drilling fluids, and pulling back conduit bundles safely.

  • 2+ years directional drill operation
  • Knowledge of ground conditions & reamer sizing
  • Class A CDL preferred
Apply for Lead Operator

Walkover Utility Locator / Tracker

Full Time

Walk bore paths with Subsite or Falcon locating systems. Guide the rig operator through tight utility easements, verify existing underground markings, check cross depths at all pothole locations, and maintain accurate bore logs.

  • 1+ years walkover tracking experience
  • Proven 811 utility awareness & safety record
  • Clear radio communication skills
Apply for Locator

Underground Laborer & Potholer

Full Time

Essential ground support for drilling crews. Operate vacuum excavation for soft potholing, set up entry and exit bore pits, handle HDPE pipe and microduct, assist with drill rod changes, and restore easements cleanly.

  • Reliable transportation & strong work ethic
  • Willingness to learn drilling and tracking
  • Great opportunity for operator apprenticeship
Apply for Laborer

Class A CDL Equipment Hauler

Full Time

Mobilize directional drills, water trucks, mud recycling trailers, and conduit spools between job sites across Illinois and regional corridors. Assist crews with jobsite staging and ground operations when mobilized.

  • Valid Class A CDL with clean driving record
  • Experience loading & strapping heavy equipment
  • Regional mobilization availability
Apply for CDL Driver
